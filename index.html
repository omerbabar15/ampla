Will one size really fit me?

It's cut long and wide on purpose, so it drapes over most bodies, from petite to plus size. On shorter wearers it falls lower like a cosy dress; on taller wearers it sits at the thigh.

How does the breastfeeding zip work?

The half zip at the chest opens wide enough to feed comfortably, and the roomy body keeps you covered and warm while you do.

What are the side zips for?

They open the hem from the bottom up, so you can sit cross-legged, drive, move freely or wear it over a bump or a baby carrier.

Is it for men too?

Yes. AMPLA is unisex, and the same size fits men and women.
